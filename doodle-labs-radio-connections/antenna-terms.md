# Antenna Terms

### Antenna Gain

The RF signal is essentially a high frequency sound. And in a similar way normal sound can be focused when coming out of a speaker, RF signal can also be focused using an antenna. When sound is focused in a certain direction, it can be louder in that direction, however it will be quiter from all other ones. Antenna gain essentially explains how much a signal is focused and amplified compared to an antenna radiating to all sides.

The choice of appropriate antenna gain is dependent on the use-case.
